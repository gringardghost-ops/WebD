Dinorwic Quarry

Dinorwic Quarry is a historic, former slate quarry located above the village of Llanberis in Eryri (Snowdonia) National Park, North Wales. it was one of the biggest Slate Quarry, and employed over 3,000 workers and produced upwards of 100,000 tons of slate annually in the late 19th century.
